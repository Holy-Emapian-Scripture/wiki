# Propriedades dos Operadores Diferenciais

1.  div, rot, $\nabla$ são lineares

2.  ${\text{div}\left( {\text{rot}(F)} \right)} = 0$ para todo campo vetorial $F:{\mathbb{R}}^{3} \rightarrow {\mathbb{R}}^{3}$

3.  $\nabla(f \cdot g) = f\nabla g + g\nabla f$

4.  $\nabla(\frac{f}{g}) = \frac{g\nabla f - f\nabla g}{g^{2}}$

5.  ${\text{div}(f \cdot F)} = \nabla f \cdot F + {\text{div}(F)} \cdot f$

6.  ${\text{div}(F \times G)} = G{\text{rot}(F)} - F{\text{rot}(G)}$

7.  ${\text{rot}(f \cdot F)} = f{\text{rot}(F)} + \nabla f \times F$

8.  ${\text{div}(\nabla f \times \nabla g)} = 0$

9.  $\mathrm{\Delta}(f \cdot g) = f\mathrm{\Delta}g + 2\nabla f\nabla g + g\nabla f$
