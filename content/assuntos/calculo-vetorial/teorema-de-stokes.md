# Teorema de Stokes

Analogamente ao Teorema de Green, temos:

$$\int_{\delta S}F \cdot d\overset{\rightarrow}{r} = \iint_{S}{\text{rot}(F)} \cdot dS$$ <a id="equation_teorema_de_stokes"></a>

Mas aqui $F:{\mathbb{R}}^{3} \rightarrow {\mathbb{R}}^{3}$.
